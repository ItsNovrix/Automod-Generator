Mass Edit Filter

Automatically removes content that has been edited using mass edit tools.

---
#  Automatically remove mass edited content
    type: any
    body+url(includes): ['redact.dev', 'codepen.io/j0be', 'github.com/j0be', 'codepen.io/pkolyvas', 'the-federation.info']
    is_edited: true
    action: remove
    action_reason: Mass Edited Content | [{{match}}]
---

Account Age Filter

Automatically removes posts and comments from accounts newer than 7 days, sending a modmail alert.

---
# Remove content from accounts newer than 7 days
author:
    account_age: "< 7 days"
action: remove
action_reason: "Account is too new (Spam Prevention)"
modmail_subject: "New Account Alert"
modmail: |
    A post/comment by /u/{{author}} was removed because their account is younger than 7 days.
    
    Link: {{permalink}}
---

Low Karma Filter

Filters content to the modqueue for review if the user has less than 50 combined karma.

---
# Filter low karma users to modqueue
author:
    combined_karma: "< 50"
action: filter
action_reason: "Low Karma User (Review Required)"
---
